Frequently asked questions

When is the right time to act?

Before the crisis emerges. Adequate arrangements exist precisely to monitor the business regularly, even in good times. At the first signs of strain (late payments, difficulties with banks, falling liquidity) it is important to act immediately: the earlier you act, the more tools are available.

How does the assistance work?

The situation is analysed with the directors, the most suitable tool is identified and negotiations with creditors and banks are conducted.

Would you like to address financial strain in good time?

A phone call or an email is all it takes to get started. I usually reply within one business day.
