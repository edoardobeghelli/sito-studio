2. Data processed

  • Browsing data. To run and protect the website, the hosting provider automatically logs technical data about requests: IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and operating system.
  • Visit statistics. The website uses Umami, an analytics service that uses no cookies and does not store your IP address: for each visit it generates an anonymous identifier through a hash function with a periodically changing value. It records, in aggregated form, the page viewed, the referring site, the type of device, browser and operating system, the approximate country and city, and the length of the visit. This data does not identify visitors and is not used for profiling.
  • Data you provide. If you contact me by email, PEC, phone or WhatsApp, I process the data you choose to share: name, contact details and the content of your request. Please do not send special categories of data (such as health data) or unnecessary documents in your first message.

3. Purposes, legal bases and retention

  • Operation and security of the website (browsing data): the controller's legitimate interest in providing a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are kept by the hosting provider only as long as strictly necessary for these purposes, unless needed to investigate unlawful activity.
  • Aggregated visit statistics: the controller's legitimate interest in understanding how the website is used in order to improve it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Statistical data is kept for 6 months.
  • Replying to enquiries and pre-contractual steps requested by you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Data is kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and, if no engagement follows, deleted within 12 months.
  • Performing any professional engagement and complying with related legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). Data is kept for the duration of the engagement and thereafter for the applicable limitation periods and the periods required by tax and professional rules. A specific notice is provided when an engagement is agreed.

Providing contact data is optional, but without it I cannot reply to your enquiry. No automated decision-making or profiling takes place.

4. Cookies and tracking tools

This website sets no cookies, technical or profiling, and uses no tracking tools such as pixels, local storage or persistent identifiers. The Umami statistics described in section 2 use no cookies, store nothing on your device and provide only aggregated data: for this reason no consent is required and no cookie banner is shown. Fonts and images are hosted on the website itself: during normal browsing the only external service your browser connects to is the analytics service.

Google map. The Contact section offers a Google Maps map, which is not loaded automatically. It loads only if you click "Load Google map" after reading the notice next to the button: that click is your consent to loading the content. Your browser then connects to Google's servers; Google receives data such as your IP address and may set its own cookies, acting as an independent controller under its own policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Consent applies to the current visit only: your choice is not stored and, if you reload the page, the map is not shown until you click again.

5. Links to third-party services

The website contains plain links to Google Maps, WhatsApp and LinkedIn. No data is sent to these services until you click a link; from that moment your data is processed by Google, Meta and LinkedIn as independent controllers, under their own policies.

7. Transfers outside the European Union

Use of the hosting service may involve transferring browsing data to the United States. GitHub, Inc. participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR) and, in addition, on the standard contractual clauses that the provider states it adopts (Art. 46 GDPR).

Umami Software, Inc. is based in the United States but stores statistical data on servers in the European Union. Any access from the United States concerns only aggregated data that does not identify visitors and is governed by the data processing agreement with the provider.
