Profile

Avv. Edoardo Beghelli

I graduated in Law from the University of Bologna with a thesis in commercial law. After training at a boutique law firm, I was a salary partner in law partnerships, working on corporate and civil law, contracts, compliance, banking law and business crisis.

I have worked alongside advisors in strategic and financial consulting and in real estate, which is why I also assess every legal issue in light of its economic implications.

Today I practise independently from my office in Bologna and assist clients throughout Italy. Outside the office I do long-distance triathlon, after many years of competitive martial arts.

The process

From first contact to engagement

  1. First contact

    A phone call, an email or a message, with a few lines about the situation.

  2. First meeting

    In person or by video call, to understand the situation, the timing and the possible options.

  3. Written quote

    A written quote setting out the work and the fees. Work starts only once it is clear and agreed.

  4. The engagement

    Regular updates on progress and direct discussion of the decisions to be made, through to completion.

Frequently asked questions

How does the first meeting work?

It is an opportunity to understand the matter and to assess together whether and how to proceed, with no obligation to instruct me. If an in-depth analysis is already needed, we agree the fee beforehand. It helps to have a short description of the facts and the main documents, such as contracts, articles of association or correspondence; if it is unclear which ones, we can discuss it by phone first.

Is the information I provide confidential?

Yes. All information received is protected by professional secrecy and by the duty of confidentiality laid down in the Italian law on the legal profession and in the Code of Conduct for Lawyers, even if no engagement follows.

How are fees set?

With a written quote, before any work begins. Depending on the engagement, fees may be agreed as a fixed fee, on an hourly basis or, for ongoing assistance, under a periodic arrangement.

Can you provide ongoing assistance to a business?

Yes. For businesses that want a stable legal point of reference I provide ongoing assistance on contracts, corporate matters and regulatory obligations. Where the business has an in-house legal team, I can support it on specific matters, with opinions and reviews, or at times of peak workload.
